title: "Aphantasia in adults"
sections:
  - title: "What is Aphantasia?"
    text: "For people with aphantasia, it is hard or even impossible to create pictures in their minds eye. For example, someone with aphantasia would not be able to see a clear picture of an apple in their minds eye, even though they know what an apple looks like. Further information about aphantasia can be found on our [FAQs](/faq) (frequently asked questions) and also on our pages for [parents](/parents) and [teachers](/teachers). The science and treatment of misophonia is described on our page for (researchers)[/researchers]."
  - title: "Can I volunteer to take part in research?"
    text: "If you are an adult (18 years or over) with aphantasia there are opportunities to take part in our ongoing research. Most of our research takes place via online surveys and tests, which you can fill out from home in your own time. See the [Test Me page](/tests) to join our research."
